Serial port

Pinout

    

      
      

        
1 · 5VPower output –x– ⚠️ do not connect to the external device

        
2 · IO1TXD (output) → external device RXD

        
3 · IO2RXD (input) ← external device TXD

        
4 · IO3OTG_ID (input) –x– unused (tied to GND inside the cable)

        
5 · GNDSignal ground ↔ external device GND

      

    

    The original UART pinout of The Pill by Shelly is compatible with standard micro-USB serial consoles, using the same pin mapping as the AP-CBL-SERU (JY728A) cable – e.g. the console port of Aruba access points (2 RXD, 3 TXD, 4/5 GND).
  

  
Serial port settings

    

      

      

      

    

    
    Off = standard console pinout (pin 2 TXD, pin 3 RXD) for a straight cable such as AP-CBL-SERU. On = pin 2 RXD, pin 3 TXD, for devices wired the other way round or for two Pills on a straight cable.
    
    
    
    "As sent by terminal" is fully transparent (binary transfers such as XMODEM). The other modes turn any Enter into exactly one CR, CR LF or LF.
